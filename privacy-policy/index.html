2. Collection and Storage of Personal Data

When you visit our website, certain general information is automatically collected. This includes data such as your IP address, browser type, date and time of access. https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en

We embed videos from YouTube, a service provided by Google. When you play these videos, data such as your IP address and usage behavior may be transmitted to Google. This happens even if you do not have a YouTube account or are not logged in.

For more details on data usage by YouTube, please see:
https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en
